Possible Insomnia Detected

Based on the health information you entered, our Random Forest model predicts a sleep pattern consistent with insomnia.

Insomnia refers to repeated difficulty falling asleep, staying asleep, or obtaining refreshing sleep despite having an opportunity to sleep.

⚠ Moderate Risk Warning

Insomnia may affect concentration, mood, productivity and overall quality of life if it continues for a long period.

If your sleep problems persist for several weeks, consult a sleep specialist or physician.

🌙 Consider consulting a doctor if you have

  • Difficulty falling asleep most nights
  • Frequent awakenings during the night
  • Waking too early every morning
  • Daytime tiredness
  • Poor concentration
  • Mood changes or irritability
  • Symptoms lasting more than 3 weeks

🔎 Common Insomnia Symptoms

People experiencing insomnia may notice some of the following symptoms.

✓ Difficulty falling asleep
✓ Waking repeatedly during the night
✓ Waking earlier than intended
✓ Difficulty returning to sleep
✓ Feeling tired during the daytime
✓ Difficulty concentrating
✓ Irritability or mood changes
✓ Feeling unrefreshed after sleeping
✓ Low daytime energy
✓ Worrying about being unable to sleep

RANDOM FOREST ANALYSIS

Possible Sleep Apnea Detected

Sleep apnea is a sleep disorder in which breathing repeatedly stops or becomes shallow during sleep. Because this happens while sleeping, people may not always recognise it themselves.

⚠ Important Warning

Sleep apnea can reduce oxygen levels during sleep and may increase the risk of cardiovascular and other health complications.

Please consult a qualified sleep specialist if these symptoms persist.

🚨 Seek medical advice if you have

  • Loud snoring almost every night
  • Breathing pauses during sleep
  • Waking up gasping for air
  • Extreme daytime sleepiness
  • Morning headaches
  • Difficulty concentrating

🔎 Common Sleep Apnea Signs

Some commonly reported signs associated with sleep apnea include:

✓ Loud or frequent snoring
✓ Breathing pauses during sleep
✓ Gasping or choking during sleep
✓ Excessive daytime sleepiness
✓ Morning headaches
✓ Dry mouth after waking
✓ Difficulty concentrating
✓ Feeling unrefreshed after sleeping
✓ Irritability or mood changes
✓ Frequent nighttime waking
